## Long description draft

```
Video Converter Pro converts and compresses your videos right on your
phone — no uploads, no accounts, no subscription required.

CONVERT
Switch between MP4, MOV, MKV, and WEBM in a couple of taps.

COMPRESS
Built-in presets for email, WhatsApp, and Instagram, or fine-tune your
own destination target (WhatsApp, Instagram Reel, TikTok, YouTube,
Email).

CREATE GIFS
Trim a clip and turn it into a shareable GIF, with control over frame
rate.

EXTRACT AUDIO
Pull the audio out of any video as MP3, AAC, or WAV.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to a
server — not for conversion, not for compression, not ever.

NO SUBSCRIPTION TRAP
Unlock everything with a single one-time purchase. No recurring charges
required to use the app's core features.

Free tier: 5 conversions/day.
Unlock unlimited conversions, batch processing, and premium presets with
a one-time purchase.
```
