## The short version

Video Converter Pro converts, compresses, and edits your videos entirely
**on your device**. Your video and audio files are never uploaded to us
or to any third party, and the app does not require an account.

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not upload, transmit, or store your video, audio, or GIF files.
  All conversion, compression, and GIF/audio extraction happens locally
  on your device using on-device processing (FFmpeg).
- We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your
  name, email address, or other identifying information to use the app.
- We do not sell your data.

## What we do collect

### Usage analytics
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, so we can
fix bugs and improve features. This includes anonymous/aggregated events
such as:
- App opened
- Conversion started / completed / failed (with the input/output format
  and whether it succeeded — never the file itself or its contents)
- Paywall viewed
- Purchase completed

Firebase Analytics may collect device-level identifiers (such as an
advertising or install ID) and general device information (OS version,
device model, country/region) as part of providing this service. See
Google's Firebase privacy documentation for details on what Firebase
itself collects and how: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Purchases
If you buy the one-time unlock (or the optional annual plan), the
purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and purchase
validation is handled by RevenueCat. We receive confirmation that you
have an active entitlement, along with basic transaction metadata
(such as product ID, purchase date, and platform). We do not receive
your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.
See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

## Local storage on your device

The app stores a few things only on your device, never on our servers:
- A local **history** of your recent conversions (filenames, sizes, and
  where the converted file is saved), so you can find and re-share past
  conversions.
- Your **preferences** (such as your default compression preset).

You can clear your conversion history at any time from Settings. Deleting
the app removes all of this local data.

## Children's privacy

Video Converter Pro is not directed at children under 13 (or the
equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Your choices

- You can use the app's Settings screen to clear your local conversion
  history at any time.
- You can disable ad personalization or limit ad tracking through your
  device's system settings.
- You can request deletion of any analytics data associated with your
  device by contacting us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]; because analytics
  events aren't tied to an account or identity we hold, this is limited
  to what Firebase's own tools support for device-level opt-out.
